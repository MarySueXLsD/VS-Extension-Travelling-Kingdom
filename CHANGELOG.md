# Changelog

## 2.0.0 — The Kingsroad

A ground-up rebuild of the game.

### New
- **Road events.** 115 encounters with choices, costs, odds and outcomes in the spirit of *Keep Driving*, including follow-up chains and choices only certain companions can make.
- **Story.** A prologue, 17 stops with their own local stories across three regions, seven road beats with a rival duke, a lost wizard and a toad archmage, and a finale at Dragon’s Rest. After the coronation, an endless Royal Progress through procedurally named towns.
- **Companions.** 18 recruits, each with a role, a passive bonus, a bio and personal lines. Reorder who walks closest in the Court panel.
- **Resources.** Coins, rations, morale and renown; markets, inns, odd jobs, foraging and shrines; 24 trinkets; titles; 22 royal decrees.
- **Court chatter.** 140+ banter lines and exchanges that react to hunger, weather, night and party members.
- **World.** Three seasonal biomes with parallax, procedural roadside scenery, weather, local-time day and night with firelight, ambient wildlife, and new locations: an orchard, a forge, a sawmill, a churchyard, a harvest festival, a frozen ferry, a frost fair, a gloomy manor, a tourney field and a sleeping dragon.
- **Interface.** New HUD, docked story cards with typewriter text and keyboard shortcuts, Court / Journal / Settings panels, a spoiler-free route map, toasts and floating rewards, a minimal theme that follows VS Code colours, and layouts that adapt down to very narrow sidebars.
- **VS Code integration.** Status bar progress, a view badge when an event awaits, optional notifications, one-window-at-a-time journeys across multiple windows, and a reset confirmation dialog.

### Changed
- Saves move to a new format; 1.x progress (hero, companions, route, settings) migrates automatically.
- Art is now packed into curated atlases, and the old character sheet with unused frames is no longer shipped.
- The game pauses while the view is hidden and defaults to 30 fps.

## 1.0.0

- First full release: character select, 14 shuffled recruit landmarks, party walking trail, Manage roster, settings for units and minimal UI.
